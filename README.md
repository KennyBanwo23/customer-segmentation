## Customer Segmentation Analysis for an Automobile Manufacturer
The company, a prominent automobile manufacturer, has been experiencing a significant decline in sales within the young professional segment, impacting overall revenue. 
Research indicates that a lack of personalized customer experiences and the absence of tailored after-sales services are major factors driving the decline in this segment. 

## Project Overview
The main objectives of the project were:
1. Explore and analyze demographic, transactional, and address data from existing and new customers.
2. Preprocess the data to handle missing values, outliers, and duplicates, and perform necessary feature engineering.
3. Apply clustering algorithms (e.g., K-Means, DBSCAN, GMM) to group customers based on meaningful patterns.
4. Evaluate the quality of clusters using metrics like silhouette score and inertia.
5. Interpret the customer segments to understand key behaviors and characteristics.
6. Recommend targeted marketing strategies for each segment to improve customer experiences and drive sales growth.

## Data Sources
1. Transactions Data: Records of customer purchases across Australian states.
2. New Customer List: Information on recent customers visiting the brand.
3. Customer Demographics: Detailed demographic profiles including age, gender, job titles, wealth segments, and more.
4. Customer Address: Location data for existing customers.
The datasets were cleaned, merged, and enhanced through feature engineering processes, such as deriving age groups, customer tenure, and income segmentation.

## Outcome
- Performed extensive data exploration and visualization to understand customer behavior patterns.
- Successfully applied clustering algorithms to segment customers into distinct groups with meaningful characteristics.
- The customers were segmented into 4 clusters which can be visually explained by the Power BI file attached.
- Identified key segments, such as:
1. Young Professionals needing personalized after-sales service packages.
2. High-income older customers with strong brand loyalty.
3. Price-sensitive buyers requiring promotions and flexible financing options.
- Developed a Power BI dashboard visualizing:
- Cluster distribution across demographics and transaction behavior.
- State-level sales trends.
- Insights into customer value and engagement potential.
- Proposed marketing strategies:
1. Launch loyalty programs and exclusive offers for high-value segments.
2. Implement personalized communication strategies for younger customers.
3. Introduce targeted after-sales services for specific customer needs.

These insights aimed to enhance customer experience, increase retention, and reverse declining sales among strategic customer groups.
